Ki*** ★★★★★ 2026-01-08

Option: 256GB / Blue Titanium

Absolutely love this phone. The titanium finish feels incredibly premium in hand, and the camera system is a massive leap from my old phone. Night mode photos are stunning — I took some shots at a concert and they came out crystal clear. Battery life easily gets me through a full day of heavy use. The Action Button is more useful than I expected, I've set it to toggle silent mode. Only minor gripe is the weight, but you get used to it quickly. Highly recommend the 256GB option if you take lots of photos and videos.

Je*** ★★★☆☆ 2025-12-03

Option: 1TB / White Titanium

The phone itself is fine, but for the 1TB price point I expected more. The camera improvements over the 14 Pro Max are incremental at best. ProRes video is nice if you're into filmmaking, but most people won't notice the difference. Also, the phone runs warm during extended camera use or gaming sessions. Apple really needs to address the thermal management. On the positive side, build quality is excellent and the titanium frame does feel lighter than the stainless steel of previous models.
