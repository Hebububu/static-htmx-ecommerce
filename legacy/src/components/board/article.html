Self-Return Service Now Available (2026.03.10~)

Admin · 2026.03.09

We are pleased to announce the launch of our new Self-Return Service, effective March 10, 2026. This service allows customers to initiate and process returns directly through their account dashboard without needing to contact our support team.

To use the Self-Return Service, simply navigate to your order history, select the item you wish to return, and follow the step-by-step instructions. A prepaid shipping label will be generated automatically and sent to your registered email address.

Please note the following conditions for self-returns:

  • Items must be in their original condition with all tags attached.
  • Returns must be initiated within 14 days of delivery.
  • Electronics and opened personal care items are excluded from self-return.
  • Refunds will be processed within 3-5 business days after the item is received at our warehouse.

For items that do not qualify for self-return, please contact our customer support team through the Contact Us page or call us at 1-800-555-0199 during business hours (Mon-Fri, 9AM-6PM EST).

We believe this new service will make your shopping experience more convenient. Thank you for choosing our store, and we appreciate your continued support.
